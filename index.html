JobSearcher

A personal job-alert assistant

JobSearcher is a small personal tool built and used by one person, its developer, Shabbir Talib. It reads job-alert emails that the developer receives from one specific sender in his own Gmail account, scores each job against his resume, and emails him a summary.

It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.
